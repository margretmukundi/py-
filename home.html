Zesty Garlic Avocado Toast

A vibrant, nutritious breakfast that combines creamy textures with a punch of citrus and spice. Perfect for a quick morning energy boost!

Ingredients

  • 2 slices of thick-cut sourdough bread
  • 1 large ripe avocado
  • 1 clove of garlic (peeled)
  • Red pepper flakes and sea salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Toast your sourdough slices until they reach a golden-brown crispness.
  2. Rub the peeled garlic clove directly onto the rough surface of the warm toast for a subtle aromatic base.
  3. In a small bowl, mash the avocado with a pinch of salt and a squeeze of lemon.
  4. Spread the mixture generously over the toast and garnish with red pepper flakes before serving.
